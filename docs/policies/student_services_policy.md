# Student Services Policy — Documents and Certificates

**School of Technology · Office of the Registrar (Academic Section)**
**Document ref.:** SS/2026 · **Effective from:** Academic Year 2026-27 · **Supersedes:** SS/2024

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This policy lists the official documents and certificates a student may request from the University, states what each is for, how to request it, how long it takes, what it costs, and how it is collected.

1.2 It applies to current students and, for the documents in clauses 3.2, 3.3 and 3.7, to former students and graduates.

1.3 Grade cards for each semester are generated automatically on the student portal under Part V clause 8.3 of the Academic Regulations and are not requested under this policy.

## 2. Requesting a document

2.1 A request is made **through the student portal**, selecting the document type, stating the purpose for which it is required, and, where the document is to be addressed to a particular organisation, the name and address of that organisation.

2.2 **A student may have only one open request for a given document type at a time.** A request is *open* while its status is *pending* or *processing*. A student who needs several copies of the same document states the number of copies in one request.

2.3 A request is not processed while the student's fee account is *overdue* under the Fee Policy (FP/2026) clause 4.3.2, or while a disciplinary proceeding under Part VII is pending against the student, except a request for a fee statement.

2.4 The purpose stated in the request appears on the document where the document is purpose-specific (bonafide certificates and letters of no objection). A student who needs the same document for two purposes makes two requests.

## 3. Documents available

3.1 **Bonafide Certificate.** Certifies that the named student is enrolled in the stated programme, department, batch and semester. Used for passport and visa applications, bank accounts, education loans, scholarship applications, and concession passes. *Processing: two working days. Fee: nil for the first two in a semester; ₹50 each thereafter.*

3.2 **Official Transcript.** A consolidated record of all courses taken, grades and credits earned, SGPA and CGPA for every semester completed, signed by the Controller of Examinations and sealed. Used for higher-studies applications, employment and credit transfer. Transcripts are issued in sealed envelopes; an electronic transcript may be sent directly to an institution on request. *Processing: seven working days. Fee: ₹300 per copy; ₹500 for a sealed set of five.*

3.3 **Migration Certificate.** Issued once, on completion of the programme or on withdrawal, to permit enrolment in another university. Requires a no-dues clearance from the library, hostel, department and Finance Office, which the Academic Section obtains on the student's behalf. *Processing: ten working days. Fee: ₹500.*

3.4 **Fee Payment Receipt.** An official receipt or statement of fees paid for a stated semester or period, signed by the Finance Office. Used for income-tax purposes, reimbursement by an employer or a scheme, and education-loan disbursement. The portal's automatic receipt suffices for most purposes; this request is for a signed statement. *Processing: two working days. Fee: nil.*

3.5 **Bus Pass.** A certificate of enrolment in the form prescribed by the state transport corporation or the local bus service for a student concession pass, stating the route between the student's residence and the University. *Processing: two working days. Fee: nil.*

3.6 **Internship NOC.** A letter of no objection addressed to a named organisation, confirming that the student may undertake an internship there for a stated period, and that the internship does not conflict with the academic calendar. Internships during a teaching period require the approval of the Head of Department, obtained by the Academic Section, and are permitted only where the attendance requirement in Part IV can still be met. *Processing: three working days (five where the Head's approval is needed). Fee: nil.*

3.7 **Course Completion Letter.** Certifies that the student has completed all requirements of the programme, or of a stated set of courses, before the degree certificate is available; used for employment joining formalities and higher-studies admission. *Processing: five working days. Fee: ₹100.*

3.8 **Character Certificate.** Certifies the student's conduct during the programme, on the basis of the disciplinary record, signed by the Dean of Student Affairs. *Processing: three working days. Fee: nil.*

3.9 Documents not listed above — for instance a rank certificate, a medium-of-instruction certificate, or a letter for a specific foreign university — are requested as *Bonafide Certificate* with the requirement described in the purpose, and the Academic Section will advise on the portal whether it can be issued and on what terms.

## 4. Status of a request

4.1 The status of each request is shown on the student portal as one of:

4.1.1 *pending* — received and awaiting verification;

4.1.2 *processing* — verified and being prepared;

4.1.3 *ready* — prepared and available for collection under clause 5, with the date it became ready;

4.1.4 *collected* — handed over to the student or dispatched;

4.1.5 *rejected* — declined, with the reason stated on the portal (for example an overdue fee account, an incomplete request, or a document that cannot be issued).

4.2 Processing times in clause 3 are counted in working days from the day after the request is received and exclude days on which the University is closed. During the two weeks following declaration of results, and the first two weeks of a semester, transcript and bonafide requests may take up to twice the stated time; the portal shows the current expected time when the request is made.

4.3 A student whose request has remained *pending* for more than three working days beyond the stated time may write to the Academic Section through the portal; a reply is given within two working days.

## 5. Collection and dispatch

5.1 Documents are collected from the Academic Section counter, Central Block, between 10:00 and 16:00 on working days, on production of the University identity card. A student may authorise another person to collect by uploading an authority letter on the portal.

5.2 Sealed transcripts and migration certificates may be dispatched by courier within India (₹100) or abroad (at cost) to the address stated in the request, or electronically to an institution's designated e-mail address where the institution accepts electronic transcripts.

5.3 A document not collected within sixty days of becoming *ready* is filed, and a fresh request is required to obtain it.

## 6. Corrections and replacements

6.1 A student who finds an error in a document returns it within seven days and a corrected document is issued without charge. Corrections requested later are treated as a new request.

6.2 A change in the student's name or other particulars on the University record, on the basis of a legal document, is made by the Academic Section on application, and documents issued after the change carry the corrected particulars.

## 7. Fees

7.1 Fees under clause 3 are paid through the portal when the request is made; a request is not registered until payment is confirmed. Fees are not refunded for a rejected request except where the rejection is for the University's own reasons.

## 8. Review

8.1 The Registrar reviews this policy annually, and may add document types or revise processing times by notice on the portal without amendment of the policy.
