# Scholarship and Financial Aid Policy

**School of Technology · Office of the Dean of Student Affairs**
**Document ref.:** SF/2026 · **Effective from:** Academic Year 2026-27 · **Supersedes:** SF/2024

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This policy describes the scholarships and financial-aid schemes available to undergraduate students of the School of Technology, the eligibility conditions for each, the application and decision process, and the manner in which awards are paid.

1.2 Schemes are of two kinds: **University schemes**, funded and decided by the University; and **external schemes**, funded by a government or other body, for which the University verifies eligibility and forwards applications. Where an external scheme's own rules differ from this policy, the scheme's rules prevail as to eligibility and amount, and this policy governs the University's part of the process.

1.3 A student may hold more than one award in a semester, but the total of all University schemes held by one student may not exceed the tuition fee for that semester.

## 2. Schemes

2.1 The following schemes are open to undergraduate students in the Academic Year 2026-27. Amounts are per semester unless stated.

| Scheme | Kind | Amount | Principal eligibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| Merit-cum-Means Scholarship | University | ₹40,000 | CGPA 7.50 or above and family income up to ₹6,00,000 a year |
| MYSY (Mukhyamantri Yuva Swavalamban Yojana) | External (Government of Gujarat) | ₹50,000 | Domicile of Gujarat, 80th percentile or above in the qualifying examination, family income up to ₹6,00,000 a year |
| PDEU Merit Scholarship | University | ₹30,000 | CGPA 8.50 or above in the preceding semester, no backlog |
| SC/ST Post-Matric Scholarship | External (Government of India / State) | ₹35,000 | Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe certificate; income limit under the scheme |
| EWS Tuition Assistance | University | ₹25,000 | Economically Weaker Section certificate; family income up to ₹8,00,000 a year |
| Sports Excellence Scholarship | University | ₹20,000 | Representation of the University at inter-university level, or state/national participation, in the preceding year |
| Girl Child Education Grant | University | ₹15,000 | Female student; first in the family to enter higher education, or family income up to ₹4,00,000 a year |

2.2 **Merit-cum-Means Scholarship.** Awarded on the basis of both academic standing and need. Renewed each semester while the student maintains a CGPA of 7.50 and has no backlog at the time of renewal. A student who falls below the CGPA in one semester may regain the award on returning to it.

2.3 **MYSY.** Applications are made on the Government of Gujarat portal within its notified window; the University verifies enrolment and fee particulars and forwards the application. Disbursement is made by the Government to the student's bank account, after which the student pays the fee; alternatively the amount may be credited directly to the University where the scheme so provides.

2.4 **PDEU Merit Scholarship.** Awarded automatically, without application, to students in each department and semester meeting the CGPA condition, on declaration of the preceding semester's results. First-semester students are considered on the basis of the admission merit list.

2.5 **SC/ST Post-Matric Scholarship.** Applications are made on the National Scholarship Portal or the State portal, as applicable, with the University's verification. The University does not charge the tuition fee of a student whose application has been approved under the scheme until the scheme's disbursement is received, provided the approval is uploaded on the student portal before the fee due date.

2.6 **EWS Tuition Assistance.** Awarded on need, on the basis of the EWS certificate and income documents, and renewed each semester subject to the student passing all courses.

2.7 **Sports Excellence Scholarship.** Awarded on the recommendation of the Director of Physical Education for the academic year following the achievement. A student must continue to represent the University to be considered in the following year.

2.8 **Girl Child Education Grant.** Awarded for the full duration of the programme subject to the student remaining in good academic standing, that is, not carrying more than two backlogs.

## 3. General eligibility conditions

3.1 In addition to the conditions of each scheme, an applicant for any University scheme must:

3.1.1 be a registered student with status *active*;

3.1.2 have attendance of at least 75 per cent in every course in the preceding semester, or hold a condonation under Part IV of the Academic Regulations;

3.1.3 not be the subject of a disciplinary penalty under Part VII in the preceding or current semester; and

3.1.4 have submitted the income and other documents required by clause 4.3.

3.2 Family income for the purpose of this policy is the gross annual income of both parents (or guardian) from all sources in the preceding financial year, as certified by the competent revenue authority.

## 4. Application

4.1 Applications for University schemes are made on the student portal during the **application window of 16 June to 31 July 2026** for the Odd Semester and of 5 January to 31 January 2027 for the Even Semester. Applications outside the window are accepted only for schemes whose eligibility arises during the semester (for example, the Sports Excellence Scholarship after an achievement).

4.2 The PDEU Merit Scholarship needs no application (clause 2.4).

4.3 An application must be accompanied by: the income certificate under clause 3.2 (for means-based schemes); the caste, EWS or domicile certificate as the scheme requires; the preceding semester's grade card (generated automatically from the portal); a bank passbook or cancelled cheque of the student's own account; and any certificate of achievement the scheme requires.

4.4 An incomplete application is returned on the portal with the missing items listed, and may be resubmitted within the window or within seven days of return, whichever is later.

## 5. Decision and status

5.1 Applications are decided by the **Scholarship Committee**, chaired by the Dean of Student Affairs, which meets within four weeks of the close of the application window. External-scheme applications are forwarded within one week of receipt and are decided by the scheme authority.

5.2 The status of every application is shown on the student portal as one of:

5.2.1 *pending* — received and awaiting decision;

5.2.2 *approved* — the award has been made and will be paid;

5.2.3 *disbursed* — the amount has been credited to the student's fee account (University schemes) or paid to the student (external schemes);

5.2.4 *rejected* — the application has been declined, with the reason stated on the portal.

5.3 A student whose application is rejected may ask for reconsideration by the Committee within ten working days, stating grounds. One reconsideration is allowed per application; the Committee's decision on reconsideration is final.

5.4 **Effect on fees.** An award in status *pending* or *approved* does not defer the fee due date under the Fee Policy (FP/2026) clause 3.3. A student in that position should pay the fee, or apply for the instalment facility under FP/2026 clause 5, which is granted as a matter of course to a student with an *approved* award. On disbursement, any excess in the student's fee account is refunded under FP/2026 clause 7.4.

## 6. Disbursement

6.1 University scheme awards are credited to the student's fee account for the semester within two weeks of the Committee's decision, and shown on the portal as *disbursed*. Where the account is already paid in full, the amount is refunded to the student's registered bank account.

6.2 External scheme amounts are paid by the scheme authority in accordance with its own timetable; the University records the application as *disbursed* when it is notified of payment.

## 7. Continuation, suspension and recovery

7.1 A renewable award continues from semester to semester while the scheme's conditions remain satisfied; the Committee reviews continuation after each declaration of results without a fresh application.

7.2 An award is suspended for a semester in which the student is detained in any course under Part IV clause 4.5, or is found guilty of examination malpractice under Part V clause 9, or receives a disciplinary penalty under Part VII. The award resumes in the following semester if the conditions are again satisfied.

7.3 An award obtained on the basis of a false declaration or document is cancelled from the outset, the amount is recovered as a fee due, and the matter is referred to the Discipline Committee.

## 8. Information and assistance

8.1 The Office of the Dean of Student Affairs publishes the list of schemes, windows and documents at the start of every academic year and holds a help desk during the application window. Students may write to the office through the portal with questions about eligibility before applying.

## 9. Review

9.1 The Scholarship Committee reviews the schemes, amounts and income limits annually and recommends changes to the Board of Management before the start of the academic year.
