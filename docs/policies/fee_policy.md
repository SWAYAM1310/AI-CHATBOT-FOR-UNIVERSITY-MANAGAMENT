# Fee Policy

**School of Technology · Office of the Finance Officer**
**Document ref.:** FP/2026 · **Effective from:** Academic Year 2026-27 · **Supersedes:** FP/2024

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy sets out the fees payable by undergraduate students of the School of Technology, the dates by which they are payable, the consequences of late or non-payment, the instalment facility, and the rules for refunds.

1.2 Fees are charged per semester. A student is liable for the fees of every semester for which they are registered, including a semester in which they are re-registered for a course under Part V clause 5.2 of the Academic Regulations.

1.3 All amounts in this policy are in Indian Rupees. Fees are revised, if at all, only at the start of an academic year and only for batches admitted in that year; a student continues to pay the fees applicable to their batch of admission throughout the programme.

## 2. Fee structure

2.1 The semester fee for undergraduate programmes comprises:

| Head | Amount per semester |
|---|---|
| Tuition fee | ₹1,49,000 |
| Other fees (library, examination, student activities) | ₹3,500 |
| **Total — day scholar** | **₹1,52,500** |
| Hostel fee (residents of University hostels only) | ₹62,000 |
| **Total — hosteller** | **₹2,14,500** |

2.2 The hostel fee covers accommodation and mess charges for the semester. It is payable by every student allotted a hostel room, whether or not the room is occupied for the whole semester, except as provided in clause 7.

2.3 The following are charged separately, when incurred, and are not part of the semester fee: late fee under clause 4; examination revaluation fee (₹500 per course) and late examination-form fee (₹500) under Part V; document fees under the Student Services Policy (SS/2026); replacement of a lost identity card (₹200); and charges for damage to University property assessed by the Estate Office.

2.4 A caution deposit of ₹10,000 is collected once at admission and refunded, less any outstanding dues, within sixty days of the student leaving the University.

## 3. Due dates

3.1 The semester fee is payable in full through the student portal on or before the due date published in the Academic Calendar for that semester.

3.2 For the Academic Year 2026-27 the due dates are:

3.2.1 **Odd Semester 2026-27 — 12 September 2026.**

3.2.2 Even Semester 2026-27 — 15 January 2027.

3.3 The due date is the same for all students and all departments. Neither a pending scholarship application nor a pending education-loan disbursement extends the due date; see clause 6.

3.4 Payment is made online through the student portal by net banking, UPI or card. A fee receipt is generated automatically on the portal for every successful payment and may also be requested as an official document under SS/2026.

## 4. Late payment

4.1 A fee not paid in full by the due date attracts a **late fee of ₹500 for every week, or part of a week, by which payment is delayed**, from the day after the due date until the day of payment.

4.2 The status of a student's fee account for a semester is shown on the portal as one of the following:

4.2.1 *paid* — the full semester fee has been received;

4.2.2 *partial* — part of the semester fee has been received, including where the first instalment under clause 5 has been paid;

4.2.3 *unpaid* — nothing has been received for the semester and the due date has not passed by more than thirty days;

4.2.4 *overdue* — the fee, or a balance of it, remains unpaid **more than thirty days after the due date** (that is, from 13 October 2026 for the Odd Semester 2026-27).

4.3 **Consequences of an overdue account.** While a student's fee account is *overdue*:

4.3.1 no hall ticket is issued for the end-semester examination (Part V clause 7.2);

4.3.2 no document request is processed under SS/2026, other than a fee statement;

4.3.3 the student may not register for the following semester; and

4.3.4 hostel residents receive a notice to vacate at the end of the semester unless the account is cleared.

4.4 A student whose fees remain unpaid at the end of the semester following the one in which they fell due is removed from the rolls, and may be readmitted only on payment of all dues with late fees and a readmission fee of ₹5,000, subject to the approval of the Dean (Academics).

4.5 The Finance Officer may waive the late fee, in whole or in part, where the delay was caused by a failure of the University payment system, or in a case of documented hardship on the recommendation of the Dean of Student Affairs.

## 5. Instalment facility

5.1 A student may apply, before the due date, to pay the semester fee in **two equal instalments**: the first by the due date in clause 3.2 and the second within sixty days of it (11 November 2026 for the Odd Semester 2026-27).

5.2 The application is made on the portal and is approved by the Finance Officer on the basis of a declaration of family income and, where relevant, the loan sanction letter of a bank. An instalment approval is valid for one semester only.

5.3 A student holding an instalment approval whose first instalment has been paid is treated as *paid* for the purpose of Part V clause 7.2 (hall ticket) and clause 4.3 of this policy until the second instalment date. Failure to pay the second instalment by its date attracts the late fee under clause 4.1 from that date and, after thirty days, the consequences in clause 4.3.

## 6. Scholarships, fee concessions and education loans

6.1 A scholarship or fee concession awarded under the Scholarship and Financial Aid Policy (SF/2026) is credited to the student's fee account when it is *disbursed*. Until disbursement the student remains liable for the full fee by the due date.

6.2 A student whose scholarship application is *pending* or *approved* but not yet disbursed on the due date should either pay the full fee and receive the scholarship amount as a refund on disbursement, or apply for the instalment facility under clause 5, which is granted as a matter of course to a student with an approved scholarship.

6.3 Where a bank has sanctioned an education loan but not yet disbursed it, the student may submit the sanction letter through the portal before the due date; the Finance Officer then records the account as *partial* pending disbursement, for up to sixty days, and no late fee accrues for that period.

## 7. Refunds

7.1 A student who withdraws from the programme before the first teaching day of a semester receives a refund of the semester fee paid, less an administrative charge of ₹1,000.

7.2 A student who withdraws within the first fifteen teaching days of a semester receives a refund of 80 per cent of the tuition fee; within thirty teaching days, 50 per cent; thereafter no refund of tuition. Other fees are not refunded once the semester has begun.

7.3 The hostel fee is refunded on a pro-rata monthly basis if a student vacates the hostel with the permission of the Chief Warden within the first two months of the semester; thereafter it is not refunded.

7.4 A refund arising from a scholarship disbursement, a revaluation, or an overpayment is credited to the bank account registered on the student portal within thirty days.

## 8. Fee statements and disputes

8.1 The student portal shows, for each semester, the amount due, the amount paid, the date of payment and the status under clause 4.2. This is the authoritative record of the student's fee account.

8.2 A student who disputes an entry writes to the Finance Officer through the portal within thirty days of the entry. The Finance Officer replies within ten working days. An unresolved dispute may be referred to the Registrar, whose decision is final.

## 9. Review

9.1 This policy is reviewed annually by the Finance Committee before the start of the academic year. The Academic Calendar for each year publishes the due dates under clause 3.
