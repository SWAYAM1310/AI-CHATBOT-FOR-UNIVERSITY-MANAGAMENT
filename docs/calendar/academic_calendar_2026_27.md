# Academic Calendar 2026-27

**School of Technology · Office of the Dean (Academics)**
**Document ref.:** AC/2026-27 · **Approved by:** Academic Council, 28 April 2026 · **Supersedes:** AC/2025-26

This calendar lists the key dates for the Even Semester 2025-26 (closing) and the Odd Semester 2026-27 for all undergraduate programmes of the School of Technology. Dates marked as *all* apply to every programme and semester; dates marked *semester-1* apply to first-year students only. Examination windows, the fee due date and the examination form window are the dates referred to by the Academic Regulations (Parts IV and V) and the Fee Policy (FP/2026). Where a holiday falls within an examination window the examination schedule published by the Controller of Examinations prevails.

## Notes

1. The last teaching day of the Odd Semester 2026-27 is 6 November 2026; attendance eligibility under Part IV clause 4.2 is determined on that day.
2. The end-semester examination form window (12 to 20 October 2026) is also the last date for condonation applications under Part IV clause 5.5.
3. Fees for the Odd Semester 2026-27 are due in full by 12 September 2026 (Fee Policy clause 3.2.1); a late fee applies from the following day.
4. Changes to this calendar are notified on the student portal and take effect from the date of the notice.
