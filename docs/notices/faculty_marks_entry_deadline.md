# Circular: Entry of Internal Test 1 marks and attendance corrections — deadline 3 September 2026

**Office of the Dean (Academics) · Circular no. DA/C/2026/19 · Dated 26 August 2026 · For faculty and heads of department only**

Course instructors are required to enter Internal Test 1 marks for every theory course in the University examination system by **Thursday, 3 September 2026** (seven working days after the last test day, 25 August 2026, as required by AR/V/2026 clause 2.5). Marks become visible to students on the portal once entered; students may query a mark within five working days of its publication.

Instructors are also reminded that attendance for every session held must be recorded on the same day (AR/IV/2026 clause 3.1). Corrections to attendance entries older than seven calendar days now require the written approval of the Head of Department (clause 3.3); the request form is available on the faculty portal.

Heads of Department are requested to review, before 10 September 2026, the list of students in each course whose attendance is below 80 per cent, and to ensure that warnings under clause 4.7 have been issued. Department-wise compliance will be reported to the Dean on 11 September 2026.
