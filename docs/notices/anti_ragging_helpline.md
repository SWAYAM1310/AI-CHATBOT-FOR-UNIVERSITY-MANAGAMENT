# Notice: Anti-ragging measures and helpline for the Odd Semester 2026-27

**Office of the Dean of Student Affairs · Notice no. DSA/N/2026/03 · Dated 12 June 2026**

Ragging in any form, inside or outside the campus, is prohibited under the Code of Conduct and Anti-Ragging Regulations (Part VII) and the UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging, 2009. It is a punishable offence.

Every student and parent must submit the anti-ragging undertaking on the student portal before **30 June 2026**. First-year students will be briefed during the orientation programme (2 to 13 June 2026).

Anti-ragging squads will make surprise visits to hostels, canteens and common areas throughout the semester. Any student who is ragged, or who witnesses ragging, should contact the University helpline **1800-000-0000** (24 hours), the national helpline 1800-180-5522, or e-mail antiragging@sot.example.edu. Complaints may be made anonymously. The identity of a complainant is protected.

The Anti-Ragging Committee meets within 24 hours of a complaint. Penalties range from suspension to expulsion and a report to the police, as provided in Part VII clause 8.
