# Notice: Internal Test 2, Odd Semester 2026-27 — 21 to 26 September 2026

**Office of the Controller of Examinations · Notice no. COE/N/2026/11 · Dated 8 September 2026**

Internal Test 2 for all undergraduate theory courses will be held university-wide from **Monday, 21 September 2026 to Saturday, 26 September 2026**, in the windows published in the Academic Calendar (AC/2026-27).

Tests are of 60 minutes and carry 20 marks (15 per cent weightage), as per the scheme of assessment in the Examination and Evaluation Regulations (AR/V/2026). The course-wise timetable and seating plan will be published on the student portal on 15 September 2026. Sessions run in two slots, 09:00–10:00 and 11:30–12:30.

Students must carry their University identity card. Mobile phones, smart watches and any printed material are not permitted in the examination hall. A student absent from a test for a reason covered by clause 4.2 of AR/V/2026 must apply for a make-up test within three working days with supporting documents.

Course instructors are requested to enter marks in the examination system within seven working days of the test, that is by 6 October 2026.
