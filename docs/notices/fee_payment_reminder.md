# Notice: Odd Semester 2026-27 fee payment — last date 12 September 2026

**Office of the Finance Officer · Notice no. FO/N/2026/07 · Dated 28 August 2026**

All undergraduate students are reminded that the semester fee for the Odd Semester 2026-27 is payable in full through the student portal by **Saturday, 12 September 2026** (Fee Policy FP/2026, clause 3.2.1).

The amounts are ₹1,52,500 for day scholars and ₹2,14,500 for hostellers, less any scholarship or concession already credited to the fee account. Students who have been granted the instalment facility should pay the first instalment by the same date.

A late fee of ₹500 per week or part of a week applies from 13 September 2026. Accounts still unpaid on 13 October 2026 become overdue, after which the portal, hall ticket and document requests are withheld until the dues are cleared.

Payment is accepted only online through the portal (net banking, UPI or card). Students facing genuine difficulty may apply for the instalment facility before the due date. Queries: fees@sot.example.edu or the Finance Office counter, 10:00–16:00 on working days.
